Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The full-deck unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID, so it restores on any device signed in with the same account, and after reinstalling the app.

Where is my study progress stored?

On your device. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so your progress is not synced to us or to any server. Deleting the app removes your progress; your unlock can still be restored as described above.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Studying works fully offline. A connection is only needed to make or restore a purchase.
