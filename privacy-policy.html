The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to our servers — we don't operate any.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Florida Real Estate Exam Prep mobile application (the "App"), published by Velum System, operated by Mohammed Reda Benhabib ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no user accounts, no login, and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the App. We do not track you across apps or websites, we serve no advertising, and we use no third-party analytics or marketing SDKs.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the App work, the following is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it or is transmitted to us:

  • Your study progress — which flashcards you've reviewed, your self-graded answers, spaced-repetition scheduling, streaks, and per-topic readiness.
  • Your app preferences — such as your selected state, theme (light/dark), and study-reminder settings.
  • Whether you have unlocked the full question deck.

This information is kept in the App's on-device storage. It is not backed up to us. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud or Google backups) under the terms of those services, which we do not control.

3. In-app purchase and third-party services

The App offers a single one-time purchase of $29.99 (a non-consumable "full deck" unlock). We do not process payments ourselves and never see or store your payment details.

  • Apple processes the payment through the App Store and handles your billing information under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat manages purchase and unlock status on our behalf. To do this, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app-user identifier and purchase/receipt information from the App Store. It does not receive your name or email from us. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

These services process purchase data only to complete and restore your purchase and to keep your unlock working across reinstalls. We use it for no other purpose.

4. How your information is used

Because we collect no personal information, we do not use, sell, share, rent, or disclose personal information. The on-device data described in Section 2 is used solely to run the App's study features on your device.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your on-device data remains until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by using the App's "Reset progress" option or by uninstalling the App, which deletes the App's local data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for you to request that we delete.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults preparing for a professional real estate license examination and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no personal information is collected from children either.

7. Security

Your study data lives on your own device and is protected by your device's own security (such as your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID). Because no personal data is transmitted to or stored by us, there is no server-side database of yours for us to safeguard.

8. Your choices and rights

You are always in control: all of your App data is on your device, and you can view, reset, or delete it as described in Section 5. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA. Since we do not collect or hold your personal information, most such requests do not apply; if you have a question, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this address. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the App where appropriate.
